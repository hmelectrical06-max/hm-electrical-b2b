Privacy & order terms

Your request details

With your consent, HM ELECTRICAL stores your name, optional company name and email address, mobile number, project/delivery location, required date, selected materials, quantities and requirement details to respond, prepare quotations and manage your request. Please avoid including payment card details or unrelated personal documents.

Online enquiries are processed through Netlify, stored in a private Supabase database, and queued for email notification through Resend to our business inbox. Only authorised business administrators can access the enquiry dashboard. Email acceptance is not a guarantee of inbox delivery. Employee DOB, addresses, personal contacts and labour codes are excluded from this website. WhatsApp opens only when you choose it; WhatsApp processes the message under its own terms. The browser retains selected catalogue IDs and quantities locally to preserve your cart, but does not retain your customer details.

For home and business service bookings, we also process the selected service, work description, Mumbai area, address, preferred date/time and, if supplied, a work photo. Booking details and uploaded photos are stored in the private Supabase service-booking system for an authorised administrator to review and respond. Photo links in the admin dashboard expire after five minutes. A booking reference confirms request receipt only; it is not an accepted appointment. No online payment is collected.

Rates and orders

Rates are in INR per stated unit. Displayed material subtotals exclude GST, delivery and installation unless separately agreed. Items without a rate, or with unavailable status, require a quotation. A request acknowledgement is not order acceptance or stock reservation. We confirm rates, stock, scope, delivery, tax and payment terms before accepting an order. No online payment is collected on this website.

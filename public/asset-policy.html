Images & pricing

Catalogue drawings are original neutral illustrations, not exact model photos. Reference images require a recorded reuse licence or owner permission and a checked product-family match before inclusion. Exact model photos require exact model verification. Existing team and site photographs supplied by HM ELECTRICAL are retained. Product photos uploaded by an authorised administrator require confirmation of the exact material and ownership or permission to publish. Embedded personal image metadata is removed before publication.

Manufacturer MRP is shown only when the exact make/model, source and verification date have been recorded; otherwise MRP is on request. GST, HSN, stock, make, unit, delivery and service scope require confirmation. Sending an enquiry does not place an accepted order.

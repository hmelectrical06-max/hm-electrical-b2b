      Quotations

      Create a draft from scratch or convert a stored enquiry. Quotes do not reserve stock or confirm orders.

      Rates and GST need review

      Product prices are entered by the admin; no MRP or selling rate is assumed. GST is only calculated from verified product tax metadata or a custom service source/date you provide. Freight/other-charge GST treatment, legal terms and tax configuration must be reviewed before issuing. No email or payment is sent or collected.

        Central godown inventory

        Stock changes are recorded in an auditable ledger. Quotes do not reserve or deduct stock.

        Safe stock controls

        Only confirmed purchase/return receipts add stock; dispatch, installation issue, supplier return, damage and adjustments are recorded as separate movements. The server rejects overselling and duplicate requests. Opening stock can be entered once per product; later corrections must use adjustment.
